          how it grows

          1. plant one pick a plot in this season's patch on the home page. it's saved in your own bluesky repo.
          2. look after it water it, give it sun or talk to it once a day for its first week. neighbours can help.
          3. watch it bloom on day 7 it opens. the more care, the fuller it grows, and the likelier something rare.
          4. keep it forever when the season ends it's pressed into your herbarium, and a new patch opens.
