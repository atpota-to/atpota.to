          how it grows

          1. plant one in a friend's garden, atpotato's, or your own. it's saved in your own bluesky repo.
          2. look after it water it, give it sun or talk to it once a day for its first week. friends can help.
          3. watch it bloom on day 7 it opens. the more care, the fuller it grows, and the likelier something rare.
          4. keep it forever when a garden closes, its flowers are pressed, and stay on your page for good.
