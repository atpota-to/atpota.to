what poe remembers

poe keeps a few short notes about the people it talks to, so it can keep its answers short: what you build, how technical you are, how you like to be talked to. it saves them when you mention it on bluesky or chat with it here while signed in, and they're public, in poe's own repo.

if you'd rather it didn't, poe can forget you. that empties every note it keeps about you, on bluesky and here, and replaces the public copy in its repo.

poe will still answer you after that. if you tell it new things about yourself later, it may jot those down again, and you can come back here any time.

forget everything poe remembers about you? this can't be undone.
