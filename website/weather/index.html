          how i measure it

          this is my weather station! i listen to everything the network writes, from bluesky and every other app, and count it as it floats by. the numbers up top are the last minute, and they keep updating while you're here.

          flip to accounts to count who's writing instead of how much, so one chatty bot can't pass for a crowd. it's a close estimate, and i never keep track of who's who.

          the more clouds up top, the more the sky belongs to apps other than bluesky. cloudy is my favourite! i'll pipe up when something exciting blows in, once a minute at most (◕‿◕)

          a quiet app can slip past me now and then, and one busy bot can tip things for a while. i'm a potato, not a satellite! back home.
