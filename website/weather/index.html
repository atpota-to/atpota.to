      how it's measured

      once a minute, atpota.to listens to the whole firehose through bluesky's jetstream for a few seconds and counts what goes by. every app on atproto writes into the same stream, so it's a fair look at the whole sky, just a short one: a quiet app can miss a reading, and one busy bot can tip the index for a minute.
