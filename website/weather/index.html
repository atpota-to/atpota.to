          how it's measured

          atpotato's weather station listens to the whole firehose through bluesky's jetstream, all the time, and counts what goes by. every app on atproto writes into the same stream, so it's a fair look at the whole sky. the numbers up top are the last 60 seconds, and the station writes down every minute for the trends.

          the clouds up top are the diversity index: a clear sky when nearly everything is bluesky, cloudier the more records come from other apps. weather alerts pop up in the corner when an app blows in, a storm rolls in, or the sky gets more mixed.

          a quiet app can still miss a minute, and one busy bot can tip the index for a while. this page checks again every few seconds while it's open. back home.
