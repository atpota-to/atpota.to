about

a small studio with its roots in the atmosphere.

atpotato makes apps, tools and guides for the atmosphere, the open network bluesky is built on. some are useful, like aturi, which opens any atproto link in the app you actually use. some are peaceful, like anisota. and some, like flushes, are just silly. we like it that way.

it's the studio of dame: a creative technologist in appalachia by day, and three moths in a trench coat by night. dame has been on bluesky since the invite-code days of 2023, and started atpotato in 2025 to give all the little experiments somewhere to grow.

poe, the potato in the corner, helps out. ask it anything about atproto.

a note from the gardener

hi, i'm dame.

atpotato is my little studio. i make apps, tools and guides for the atmosphere, the open network bluesky is built on, and i try to make each one feel a little handmade, and a little mischievous.
