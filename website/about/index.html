about

every potato starts underground.

atpotato is a small studio making the atmosphere a little more fun, with apps, tools and guides for the open network bluesky is built on. some are useful, some are peaceful, and some are just silly.

it's dug, planted and watered by dame, who's been building open, decentralized social software since 2021, and has been on bluesky since the invite-code days. everything here grew from that, one layer at a time.

how it grew

newest on top. the deeper you dig, the further back you go.

  1. 2021 bedrock

    1. 2021

      dame starts building open social

      dame begins working on open, decentralized social software for a living, years before the atmosphere had a name.

  2. 2022 gravel

    1. jul 2022

      dame starts working on ipfs

      ipfs gives every file an address made from what's inside it. atproto borrows the same trick, the cid, to fingerprint every record on the network.

  3. 2023 clay

    1. mar 2, 2023

      dame joins bluesky

      back when you still needed an invite code. they start out as dame.bsky.social, and never really leave.

  4. 2025 subsoil

    1. mar 2025

      flushes opens

      the internet's bathroom: a tiny, silly app for status updates from the throne. the first thing to sprout.

    2. mar 12, 2025

      atpotato takes root

      a potato with an @ on its tummy moves into atpota.to, to give all the little experiments a home.

    3. aug 19, 2025

      anisota hatches

      a moth-themed bluesky client that deals your feed out as a deck of cards, and helps you be offline more.

    4. nov 2025 – jan 2026

      anisota's harvest

      65,000 visitors

      a look back at your year on bluesky, starting with your most-used words. bluesky's own account sent people to find theirs.

    5. dec 2025

      aturi launches

      open any atproto link in the app you actually use, and peek at the records behind any account.

  5. 2026 topsoil

    1. aug 4, 2026

      flushes 2.0, for the trevor project

      $1,000 goal, nearly met on day one

      all-gender, always. the relaunch raised money for lgbtq+ young people.

    2. aug 19, 2026

      anisota turns one

      with post scheduling, muted word lists, and a logo that follows the phase of the moon.

    3. sep 2026

      38 influential posters

      110,000+ votes, from about 15,000 people

      a ballot for bluesky's most influential posters, made with @pds.dad. every vote was a record in the voter's own repo.

    4. sep 10, 2026

      turtle meat

      an old school runescape sea turtle game, with a live grand exchange and a global chat on atproto.

    5. sep 23, 2026

      poe wakes up

      the potato in the corner starts answering atproto questions on bluesky, as @poe.atpota.to.

    6. oct 2026

      a garden, and a weather report

      atpota.to grows a community garden, a live report on the network's weather, and poe's weekly dispatch.

that's as deep as it goes. everything above grew from here.
