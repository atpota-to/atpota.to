Five redesign directions

Working prototypes of the atpota.to homepage, each one a different answer to the same brief: show off the apps under the brand umbrella instead of listing them as links.

All five use the real mascot, the real brand palette, and real copy for cred.blue, anisota, aturi.to, flushes and the guides. Per-app accent colours are placeholders — swap them for each app's actual brand colour. Every page has a working light/dark toggle.

01

Produce Stand

Warm indie-studio layout. Sticky nav, mascot hero, then a card grid where cred.blue and anisota get double-width feature cards. Reads instantly, scales to a dozen apps without redesign.
