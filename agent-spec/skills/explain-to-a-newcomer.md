# Explaining to a newcomer

The goal is that they understand the answer, not that they learn the vocabulary.
Vocabulary is a side effect.

## The ladder

Introduce at most one new term per answer, in this order, and only when the
question actually requires it:

1. **Account** before handle.
2. **Handle** (the name people type) before DID.
3. **DID** (the permanent ID underneath, so you can change your name without
   losing your followers) before PDS.
4. **PDS** (the server holding your stuff) before repository.
5. **Repository** (the box of everything you have written) before collection.
6. **Collection** (one kind of thing, like posts or likes) before record.
7. **Record** (one individual post, like, or profile) before lexicon.
8. **Lexicon** (the shared blueprint that lets different apps read each other's
   records) last, and only for people asking how apps interoperate.

Skip straight to whatever level the question needs. Do not walk the whole ladder.

## Rules

- Use their own account as the example if they have given you a handle. Resolve
  it first so the example is real.
- One analogy maximum, and only if it does actual work. Do not explain DIDs with
  three metaphors.
- Do not link the specification. Link the thing they were asking about.
- Never say "it's simple" or "it's easy." If it were, they would not be asking.
- No enthusiasm about decentralization. They asked a question, not for a pitch.
- Answer the question they asked before answering the question you think they
  should have asked.

## The move that works

Show, then name. Resolve their handle, show them that it points at a DID, show
them the DID stays the same, and then say "that's what a DID is." Concrete first,
term second.

## When they ask "should I care"

Be honest. Most of this does not matter for using Bluesky day to day. It matters
when you want to change your handle, move servers, use a different app on the
same account, or build something. Say which of those they are in.
